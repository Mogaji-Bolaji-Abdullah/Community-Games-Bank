EVERYONE CAN PLAY

How to play Crowd Says!

Two teams guess the hidden answers. The host reveals matches, tracks points and decides the winner.
01

Choose your teams

Make 2–8 teams. Choose ready-made questions, your own, or a mix. Pick the number of rounds and a timer. Put the separate audience screen on your projector.

02

Face-off & take control

The host reads the prompt. One representative from each of the two selected teams gives an answer. The host determines who wins the face-off and selects the team that will play the board.

03

Reveal or strike

Players take turns guessing an answer on the board. The host clicks the matching answer privately to reveal it. Incorrect or repeated answers earn one strike. After three strikes, the rival team gets one steal attempt.

04

Steal the round

The rival team gives just one answer. If it matches an unrevealed answer, they win the entire round pot. If not, the original team keeps the pot. Clearing the whole board also wins the round.

05

Points & winner

Each revealed answer adds its displayed play points to the round pot. Round 1–2: 1×; rounds 3–4: 2×; rounds 5+: 3×. The team with the most total points at the end wins.

06

Host rules

The host has the final say on close answers, timing and disputes. Keep answers secret, take turns, respect all cultures and participants, and play fair. Timers do not automatically issue strikes.

Important: The included question cards are original, curated prompts with illustrative play-point values — not results from 100 surveyed people. This is an independent event game, not affiliated with the Family Feud TV franchise. The audience display syncs only between windows on the same device/browser; it is not a multi-device online room.
