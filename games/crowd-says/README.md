# Crowd Says!

A free survey-board guessing game built for community events, team socials, learning programmes and family gatherings. **Independent game, not affiliated with any TV show.**

## Play

Open [Crowd Says](https://community-games-bank.vercel.app/games/crowd-says/). Add 2–8 team names, choose a question pack and timer, then start. Two teams play each round. The host reads the prompt, reveals matching answers and decides on close guesses. Three strikes give the other team one chance to steal the pot. There is also a bonus round. Open the **Audience screen** on the same device for a projector.

## Questions

Includes **120 curated questions**: 40 Nigerian, 40 African and 40 global-themed. Answers are assigned illustrative play points; they are **not actual survey results**.

Create your own question in the setup screen with 4–8 unique `Answer | points` lines. Select **My questions** to use only your own, **Ready-made** for ours, or **All cards** to mix both. Export or import your question packs as JSON to reuse on another device. Custom questions are stored only in your browser.

## Tech

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript — no installation or backend. The host controls everything; the audience view synchronizes with windows on the same browser/device, not across other devices. Browser data can be cleared; export your question packs for backup.
