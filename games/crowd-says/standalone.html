How to run the showdown.

For community managers, facilitators and event hosts. You run one laptop; teams answer aloud and the crowd watches the big board.

01

Make two teams. Choose names, select Nigeria, Africa or Global packs and decide how many rounds to play. A host or MC operates the buttons.

02

Face-off. Read the question. Invite one player per team to answer; reveal any matching answers. The player with the higher-ranked answer wins control, then chooses play or pass.

03

Play the board. The controlling team takes turns guessing. The host reveals answers and awards their displayed points to a round pot. A wrong answer is one strike.

04

Three strikes = steal. The opposing team gets ONE final guess. If it reveals a new correct answer, they take the whole round pot. If not, the original team banks it. Clearing the board banks the pot immediately.

05

Count the points. Rounds 1–2 are ×1, rounds 3–4 are ×2, and rounds 5+ are ×3. After the last round, the highest total wins. Try the optional two-player Bonus Dash.

Fair-play rules: No phones or audience hints; teams answer within the host's time limit; the host accepts reasonable synonyms and settles disputes. Unrevealed answers are never shown on the projector. The points are fictional, editorial play values — not results from a real survey.
