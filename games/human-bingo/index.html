      HOW TO PLAY

      Make connections, one square at a time.

      1. Get your game room code from the organizer.
      2. Join the game using your name and the room code.
      3. Read the prompts on your bingo card.
      4. Walk around and talk to other participants.
      5. Find one person who matches each prompt.
      6. Ask for their player code, tap the matching square and enter it.
      7. Use a different person for every square.
      8. Complete the full card, or one full line if the organizer chose line mode.

      Be friendly, respect everyone's privacy, and never pressure anyone to answer a question they are uncomfortable with.
