03 / THE IDEA

Less planning.
More playing.

Every community manager knows the pressure of getting a room to connect. We built Community Games Bank so the fun part doesn't become another task on your to-do list.

This is a free, open-source collection for facilitators, community builders, volunteers and event hosts. We're starting with Charades and building the rest with you.
